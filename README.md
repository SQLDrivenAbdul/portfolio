### [Sunrise Coffee Chain: Regional Profitability Analysis](https://github.com/SQLDrivenAbdul/Sunrise-Coffee-Chain-Regional-Performance-Analysis)

Conducted an end-to-end profitability analysis of a fictional UK coffee retail business operating 60 stores across 12 regions. Using SQL Server, I analysed regional and store-level financial performance, investigating revenue, operating expenses, and profit margins to uncover the factors driving profitability differences. The analysis revealed that London generated the highest revenue but recorded the lowest profit margin (8.45%), with rental expenses emerging as a key cost driver. I also investigated individual store performance to identify cost inefficiencies and developed data-driven recommendations to support operational improvements and the company's 2025 expansion decisions.
